## 1. Project in one paragraph

THOCK is a Roblox simulator about mechanical keyboards, in the style of Steal a Brainrot.

- **Core loop:** players stand in their base and roll (tap the big key) to get random keyboard keys. There are 7 rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic and Secret.
- **Income:** keys placed on the base board earn Clicks, the currency.
- **Stealing:** players steal keys from other bases by holding E.
- **Systems:**
  - upgrades with x1/x10/MAX buying
  - Golden Roll every N rolls
  - glass walls and a laser lock for defense
  - a Market stall
  - playtime GIFTS
  - a group chest
  - Robux store, passes and a season pass
  - rebirth, codes, leaderboards and trading
  - the obby, which is currently **disabled**

- **Code style:** strict Luau and **no code comments**.
  - Every service and controller uses Init/Start/Destroy and cleans up with Trove.
  - Networking goes through `src/shared/Packets.luau` with rate limits.

- **Contract files:** `src/shared/Packets.luau`, `src/shared/Config/*`, `src/shared/Rules/*` and `src/server/Data/PlayerData.luau`. Change them deliberately, and make additions backward compatible because ProfileStore reconciles new fields.

  - No neon, because it's too bright.

### D. Steal rework

1. **Steal prompt:** only the best stealable key within range shows its "Steal!" prompt. Toggle this client-side in StealController.
2. **Knocking down a thief:** replace the tag mechanic.
   - When a thief carries your key, you (the victim only) see a "Knock down!" prompt (E) on them.
   - **Ragdoll:** the thief ragdolls for 2.5s.
   - **Dropped key:** the key drops as a spinning pickup labelled "OWNER: name". Only the owner can "Take back" (hold 0.5s). If nobody takes it within 25s, it returns to the owner automatically, and the thief can't re-grab it.
   - **Cooldown:** a thief can't be knocked down again for 3s.
3. **Walls:** walls never teleport anyone.
   - The laser lock only knocks back players crossing the entrance laser plane. Right now `Lock.IsInside` and `Lock.Knock` eject anyone inside the whole locked base.
   - Level-1 walls must be jumpable from outside (default JumpHeight 7.2). Level 2 and up must not be.

### E. Eggs and Charms (the Market sells eggs instead of keys)

**Contracts already added:**
- PlayerData: `Incubators {"","",""}`, `IncubatorReadyAt {0,0,0}`, `Charms {[id]=count}` and `EquippedCharms {id...}`.
- Packets: `HatchEgg(u8) -> string` and `EquipCharm(string, bool) -> bool`. `MarketBuy(u8)` is reused.

**Eggs:**
- **Tiers:** Linear (2 min), Tactile (8 min), Clicky (20 min), Golden (45 min) and Rainbow (2h).
- **Price:** minutes of the player's current income, with a multiplier per tier.
- **Buying:** an egg goes straight into the first free incubator. If all 3 are full, reject the buy.
- **Incubators:** 3 pedestals at the back of each base, each with a glass dome, the egg inside and a countdown sign. When ready, the owner holds E to hatch, which plays an animation and then reveals the charm.

**Charms:**
- **What they are:** keyboard keychain charms with rarities.
- **Effects:**
  - income multiplier (up to x5, plus an x10 "Diamond Spacebar" at about 1% from the Rainbow egg)
  - luck multiplier
  - roll speed
  - golden roll every -N rolls
  - faster steal holds
  - longer laser time
- **Equipping:** equip up to 3. Wire them into `Rules/Stats` (Compute and GoldenEvery) so every system picks them up.
- **Visuals:** equipped charms float around the player.
- **Screens:** a new Charms screen with a grid, EQUIP buttons, Equip Best and the 3 equipped slots. Add a CHARMS HUD button.
- **Market screen:** rework it to show eggs, with hatch time, price, stock and drop chances.
