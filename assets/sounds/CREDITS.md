# Sound credits

The switch recordings in this folder (`thock_switches.ogg` and `clips/`) are derived from the sound packs of Mechvibes: https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes

The clips were trimmed, level-matched, faded and re-encoded; no other changes were made.
